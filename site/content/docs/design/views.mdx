The view buttons in the toolbar (Shift-Command-1, 2 and 3) change what the canvas shows. Each
one is the same live render the files are made from.

## One

One screen at a time, as large as the window allows. Option-Command-Up and Down step through the
screens.

## Strip

Every screen of the page side by side, the way the store shows them. Use it to read the page as
a visitor would, and for art and backgrounds that run on from one screen to the next.

**Live Listing** puts what the App Store shows today under your drafts, for the storefront
country you pick: the quickest way to see what a release will change.

![Strip: the drafts on top, the live App Store listing underneath](/images/app-strip-live.png)

The strip shows only the screens that make screenshots for the device shown. A screen made only
for the header, search results or event images stays out of the iPhone strip, by the same rule
Generate uses.

## Languages

One screen in every language at once. It is the quickest way to catch the translation that
runs long, the one that falls back to a smaller size, or a missing glyph.

![Languages: one screen in all seven of Braele's languages](/images/app-languages.png)
