Each screen is a template filled with copy per language, a raw capture from your app, and
whatever you put on it. Design (Command-1) has three parts:

- **Screens**, on the left: the page's screens in order. See [Screens and order](/docs/design/screens).
- **The canvas**, in the middle: the screen as the store gets it, rendered by the same code that
  writes the files. See [The canvas](/docs/design/canvas).
- **The inspector**, on the right, in tabs: [Text](/docs/design/copy),
  [Layout](/docs/design/layout), [Background](/docs/design/backgrounds),
  [Layers](/docs/design/layers) and [Screen](/docs/design/screens#the-screen-tab).

![Design: the screens on the left, the canvas, and the inspector](/images/app-design.png)

## The toolbar

The toolbar picks what the canvas shows:

- **Page**: the default product page, or a custom product page or test. A page shows its own
  screens in its own order.
- **Device**: every device the app renders for, from iPhone 6.9" to the Google Play feature
  graphic.
- **Language**: every language of the app. Command-[ and Command-] step through them.
- **View**: one screen, the strip, or every language. See [Views](/docs/design/views).
- **Zoom**: Command-= in, Command-- out, Command-0 actual size, Command-9 fits.

**App Store look** shows the screens as the store does, on a dark page with rounded corners.
**Guides** (Option-Command-G) draws the safe padding, centres and thirds and, for header images,
the two crops App Store Connect makes.

## The status line

The foot of the canvas runs the checks a build runs, as you type: text that overflows at its
smallest allowed size, text over the device, missing glyphs, missing copy, a missing capture.

## Saving and undo

Edits save themselves a moment after you stop, into the app's own files. Command-Z undoes them,
one step per change you made, not per keystroke. Unsaved edits are written before the engine
restarts or the app quits.

## Go anywhere

Command-K opens a palette that jumps to any app, section, screen, page, language or device, or
runs an action (generate, switch the view, open the log), by typing part of its name.
