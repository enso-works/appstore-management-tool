---
title: What it is
description: App Store and Google Play screenshots, built from your app's real captures and checked against the rules that get listings rejected.
---

store-shots is a native Mac app over an open-source engine that runs on your machine. There
is no account, and nothing is uploaded unless you ask for it.

![Rallo's App Store listing: eight landscape screenshots in one stadium, a 3D player in front of each phone](/images/hero.png)

That listing is one app in the tool: the game's own captures in a real iPhone body, a 3D player
rendered from the game's models in front of each phone, and a stadium backdrop that runs on from
one screen to the next.

Making an image look good is the easy half. The other half is knowing, before you upload, that
the German headline no longer fits at the smallest allowed size, that Arabic needs a fallback
font for three glyphs, that the keywords are 104 bytes in Japanese, and that a caption still
says "50% off". The tool checks all of that while you edit, and again before anything ships.

## Four sections per app

- **Design** every screen on a canvas that is the exact render the store gets.
- **Pages**: the default product page, custom product pages, optimization tests, the iOS 27
  header and search results images, and iPhone Duo sets.
- **Listing**: name, subtitle, keywords and the rest of the store text, per language, with the
  store's limits.
- **Ship**: readiness checks with the fix for each, a review of every file, fastlane uploads,
  and drafts in App Store Connect.

## Three ways to run it

- **The Mac app**, for everyday work. It starts the engine itself.
- **The command line**, for CI and for working without a window. Everything the app does, the
  CLI does too.
- **The browser editor** (`store-shots open`), the same canvas in a browser, for when you are
  not on a Mac.

## Where your work lives

Everything the tool knows about an app is in the app's own folder, next to its code, so it is
versioned with the app:

```text
store-shots.config.json    devices, languages, brand, fonts
store/                     screens, copy, captures, art
fastlane/                  store text and the generated screenshots
```
