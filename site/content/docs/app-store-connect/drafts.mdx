---
title: Pages and treatments
description: Custom product pages and test treatments, uploaded as drafts.
---

Each custom page and treatment in Pages has **Check** and **Upload Draft**.

- **Check** compares the page with what App Store Connect has and lists what an upload would
  change. **App Store Connect is up to date** means nothing.
- **Upload Draft** creates the page or treatment on the first upload, then updates it: its
  version, text, keywords, deep link and screenshots, as a draft. App Store Connect's id goes
  into the manifest, so the next upload updates the same page.

An upload refuses stale renders (generate first) and leaves a page that is in review alone.

```sh
store-shots asc push anxiety           # the plan
store-shots asc push anxiety --yes     # upload it
```

Submitting a custom page, or starting a test, stays a click in App Store Connect.
