---
title: Google Play
description: Phone screenshots and the feature graphic, written where fastlane supply uploads them.
---

Two devices cover Google Play:

- `play-phone-1080x1920`: phone screenshots. A phone capture keeps a phone's proportions on
  the 9:16 canvas.
- `play-feature-1024x500`: the feature graphic, opt-in per screen like the header images.

Import App adds them when the app already ships to Google Play through fastlane, that is when
`fastlane/metadata/android` exists. Generate writes them to
`fastlane/metadata/android/<locale>/images/`, where `fastlane supply` uploads them.
