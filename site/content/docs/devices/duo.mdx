---
title: iPhone Duo
description: The foldable iPhone's screenshot sets, required from April 2027.
---

The foldable iPhone has its own sets: `iphone-duo-1398x2034` (outer screen) and
`iphone-duo-2007x2853` (inner screen), and the same in landscape. App Store Connect requires them
from April 2027.

They render from the iPhone captures by default. With captures from a Duo simulator, put them in a
folder of their own and point the Duo sets at it with `sourceDevices` in the config:

```json
"sourceDevices": { "iphone-duo-1398x2034": "duo", "iphone-duo-2007x2853": "duo" }
```

The Duo sets then read `store/raw/duo/<locale>/` instead of the iPhone captures.

fastlane has no display type for them yet, so they render into `store/generated/duo/<locale>/`
and upload with the rest of the default page from **Pages** (`store-shots asc push default`),
not through deliver.
