---
title: In-app events
description: The event card and details image, made from the same captures.
---

In-app event media render from the app's captures: `event-card-1920x1080` (the card in search
and on the product page) and `event-detail-1080x1920` (the event's own page). They show the
app's own iPhone capture, whichever way round the app runs.

Like the header images, they are opt-in per screen: add the device in **Settings**, then a
screen that names it under **Devices**. They render into `store/generated/events/`, to add to the
event in App Store Connect.
